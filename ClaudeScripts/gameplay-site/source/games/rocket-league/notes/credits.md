## Built here (no third-party files)
- Merc, Breakout, Venom and Paladin bodies: procedural, generated by `tools/gen_<car>.py` (shared helpers in `tools/cargen.py`) from the garage reference shots.
- Antennas (Soccer Ball, Smiley, Rocket Flag, Green Leaf, Macaron, Skull), toppers, alternative wheels, boosts: three.js primitives in `seg/07_cars.js`.
- Ball: procedural (arena agent, `seg/06b_ball.js`).

## Searched and rejected (round 2)
Merc, Breakout, Venom and Paladin: no model with a clear redistributable licence that is not a game rip.
- Cults3D / Thingiverse "Rocket League (Series)" by wscarlton (Merc, Breakout STL, CC BY No AI): author states they are in-game models.
- Printables "Rocket League Cars" / "Breakout" by BigBoyBob: ripped game files.
- Sketchfab "Rocket League Car - Breakout" by ROCKET LABS: CC BY label but appears to be the game mesh and downloads need a login.
- No Paladin or Venom model found under CC0 / CC BY / MIT.

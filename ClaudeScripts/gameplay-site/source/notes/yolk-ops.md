# Yolk Ops: study notes (cartoon egg browser shooter)

Model: the cartoon egg shooter played on school laptops (egg characters, cracking eggs instead of blood, weapon classes, free-for-all and teams). Sources: the public wikis (shellshockers.fandom.com weapon pages for the rifle, shotgun, marksman rifle and sniper), weapon guides and gameplay footage. Names, eggs, maps, sounds and art in Yolk Ops are all new.

## Session loop and pacing
- Pick a weapon class and a mode, press Play, spawn in a running game. Die, see the egg crack, respawn after about 5 s with a button (change class first if you like).
- Free-for-all and team games run with a score target and a timer; matches are short and drop-in.
- Kill streaks earn a reward you chose before (a hard shell, a health refill, double ammo).

## Maps
- Blocky toy-like maps of about 60 to 90 m: crates, ramps, stairs, bridges, two or three height levels, tunnels under the main floor. Lots of jumping: eggs jump high and float a little.
- Ammo boxes and grenades lie on the map and respawn after a while.
- Fights centre on raised middles and bridges; snipers hold long lanes.

## Controls and numbers (relative)
- WASD, mouse look, left click fire, right click or Shift aims (scopes for the sniper), R reload, Space jump, E or Q swaps to the pistol, G or middle click throws a grenade, F melee. No sprint, no crouch slide.
- 100 health. Health comes back slowly after a few seconds without damage.
- Weapons: auto rifle about 30 damage at close range falling off to little at range, 30 rounds; two-shot shotgun up to about 150 up close; marksman rifle about 100 to the centre of the egg, 15 rounds; sniper one shot to the centre of the egg, almost nothing on the edge, scope; SMG fast and weak; rocket launcher with splash; burst rifle three-round bursts. Pistol secondary for everyone; up to three grenades.
- Damage depends on distance (falloff), and the centre of the egg takes more than the edge.

## Interface
- Health shown as a ring or bar near the bottom, ammo and grenades bottom right, kill feed top right, scoreboard top left, streak counter, chat. Hit markers, cracking sound on a kill, yolk splat when an egg breaks, a shield effect when hard-shelled.

## Feel
- Bright, toy-like and funny: eggs wobble when they walk, hands and gun float in front of the egg, guns are chunky, deaths are a crack and a splat with shell pieces, never blood.
- Big floaty jumps, medium walking speed, soft recoil on the rifle and a big kick on the sniper and shotgun.

## Strategies and beginner mistakes
- Good players jump while fighting to dodge, take high ground, pick up grenades and ammo, aim for the centre of the egg, switch to the pistol instead of reloading.
- Beginners stand still, fire the sniper unscoped, run out of ammo and forget grenades.

## Decisions for Yolk Ops
- Weapon classes (original names): Sunny Rifle (auto rifle), Poacher (two-shot shotgun), Over Easy (marksman rifle), Benedict (sniper), Whisk (SMG), Souffle (rocket launcher), Triple Yolk (burst rifle). Pistol "Peep 9" for everyone, grenades, a spatula melee.
- Modes: Free for all, Teams, King of the Coop (hold the coop zone); bots Easy, Normal, Hard, Hardest; online through the party system with bots filling seats.
- Streak rewards: Hard Shell (armour), Fresh Shell (full health), Full Carton (refill ammo and grenades).
- Three original maps: Barnyard (barn, silos, hay bales, a bridge over a creek), Sunnyside Docks (containers, cranes, a ship deck), Diner Rooftops (city roofs joined by planks).

# Touchdown Dynasty: study notes

Reference: the retro pixel American football management game of the Retro Bowl kind. Title, team names, players, art, sounds and the championship (the Golden Bowl) are this game's own.

## Loop and pacing
- New career: pick one of 32 teams (two conferences, four divisions), a coach, quarter length and difficulty. Then the hub: next game, roster, free agents, facilities and staff, standings, schedule, news.
- Game day: you play only the offense; the opponent's drives are simulated and shown as a short play-by-play. A game has 4 to 7 own drives; quarter length 1:30 to 4:00 of game clock.
- Season: 17 weeks, then Wild Card, Divisional, Conference Final and the final. Offseason: retirements, contract renewals, a draft with scouted prospects, free agency.

## Controls and numbers
- Before each snap: choose a pass play (short, medium, deep), a run (inside, outside, QB draw), or on 4th down a field goal or punt.
- Passing: receivers run routes; tap a receiver (auto lead) or drag to aim; a longer hold behind a collapsing pocket means a sack. Running: steer, juke (short burst, cooldown about 1 s), dive.
- Kicks: hold to charge, aim against the wind; field goals from about 50 yards are uncertain.
- 100-yard field, 10 yards for a first down, 4 downs; a play burns 5 to 16 s of clock; incompletions stop the clock.

## Interface
- Top bar: score, quarter and clock, down and distance and ball spot. Under the field: the play calls with a one-line description, then the receivers to throw to, then juke and dive while running.

## Management feel
- Salary cap around 150M with player salaries by position and rating; morale per player (low morale makes drops and misses more likely); coaching credits from wins buy facilities, staff and free agents; the draft is the cheap way to build.

## Common strategies and beginner mistakes
- Short passes and the right read beat forcing deep balls into coverage; throwing late into a blitz gives interceptions.
- Beginners overpay veterans, ignore morale and skip the medical facility, then lose stars to injury.

## Hardest
- Same ratings, speeds and rules as the player's opponents on Pro. The defence reads the play 0.15 to 0.25 s after the snap, man defenders react with 0.15 to 0.25 s lag and anticipate routes from what they saw, defenders break on a thrown ball after 0.17 s, pursuit takes intercept angles, the coach learns which coverage works against you over the season, and the opposing offence picks plays by matchup, goes for it on short 4th downs and manages the clock.

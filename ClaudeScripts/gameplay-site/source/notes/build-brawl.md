# Build Brawl: study notes (build-and-shoot duel, in the style of 1v1.LOL)

Sources: public guides and game portals describing 1v1.LOL (controls, modes, weapons), general knowledge of the build-and-shoot genre it copies.

## Session loop and pacing
- Menu, pick a mode, short countdown, fight, round result, next round, match result, rematch or menu.
- Build fight: both players start apart on a flat island with full materials; a round lasts 30 to 90 seconds and ends on the first elimination; a match is first to 3 or 5 rounds.
- Box fight: both players start inside one-tile boxes (four walls, floor, cone roof) in a small arena; fights are at arm's length, decided by edits (open a window, shoot, close it) and shotgun timing.
- Practice: free build with unlimited materials and targets, to drill ramp rushes and ninety-degree turns.

## Map and grid
- One building grid: a tile is about 2.7 player widths wide and a wall is about 2 player heights tall, so a player cannot jump over a wall without building.
- Four pieces: wall (on a tile edge), floor (on a tile), ramp (on a tile, rising in the facing direction) and cone (a pyramid roof on a tile).
- Pieces that lose every link to the ground collapse.
- Fights happen in the air: whoever holds the high ground (ramp rush, then wall in front) usually wins.

## Numbers (relative)
- Health 100 and shield 100; eliminations in free-for-all give health and shield back.
- Run speed about 5.5 tiles per 5 seconds (5.5 m/s for a 1.8 m player); jump about 1.3 m; quick fall.
- Assault rifle: about 5 shots a second, 30 a magazine, about 30 damage, 1.5 times on the head, bloom when spamming.
- Pump shotgun: one shot every second, 5 shells, up to about 90 damage close, 2 times on the head, falls off fast.
- SMG: about 12 shots a second, low damage, best in box fights.
- Sniper: one shot, about 100 body, 2.5 times on the head.
- Rarity (common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary: grey, green, blue, purple, gold) adds about 5 percent damage per step and shortens reloads.
- Materials: wood, stone, metal, 10 per piece; pieces appear instantly at low health and grow to full (wood fastest and weakest, metal slowest and strongest).

## Interface
- Crosshair at the centre, over-the-shoulder camera on the right.
- Bottom right: weapon slots with rarity colours and ammo; build slots with their keys; material counts.
- Bottom left: health (green) and shield (blue) bars. Top: round score and timer. Kill feed top right.
- Ghost preview of the piece (blue when it fits, red when blocked); edit mode shows the piece's grid tiles.

## Controls (PC)
- WASD move, Space jump, Shift crouch, mouse aim, left click fire or place, right click aim down sights.
- 1 to 5 weapons (1 pickaxe), Z wall, X floor, C ramp, V cone, G edit (click or drag the tiles, release or G to confirm, right click resets), R reload, Q switch material.
- Phone: left stick, drag to look, fire, jump and the four build buttons.

## Feel
- Hit markers with a tick sound, white numbers for body, yellow for head, blue when shield breaks.
- A crunch and debris when a piece breaks; wood, stone and metal sound different.
- Kill: big banner, the eliminated player's model breaks apart.

## Strategy and beginner mistakes
- Wall when shot at, ramp to take height, shotgun at close range, rifle at range.
- Beginners fight on the ground, forget to reload, and run out of materials in long fights.
- Strong players edit through their own walls, take the high ground and replace walls that were shot.

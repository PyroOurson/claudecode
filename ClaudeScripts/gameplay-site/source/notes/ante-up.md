# Ante Up: study notes

The best-known game of this kind is the 2024 poker-hand deck-building roguelike. Studied from its public wiki, reviews and run guides. Ante Up keeps the idea (play poker hands for chips times mult, beat blinds, buy jokers between blinds) with content of its own: every joker, consumable, upgrade, boss, tag, deck, stake, price and number is designed for Ante Up.

## Session loop and pacing
- New run: choose a deck and a stake, then eight antes. Each ante has three blinds: small, big and a boss with a rule twist. A blind gives a fixed number of hands and discards to reach a target score; reaching it ends the blind at once. Beating the eighth boss wins the run; endless mode continues after.
- A run lasts 30 to 60 minutes. The target grows about two and a half times per ante; early antes fall to plain hands, late antes need a joker engine of multiplying effects.
- After each blind: cash out (blind reward, $1 per unused hand, interest on savings), then a shop with two cards, two booster packs and one upgrade, with paid rerolls. Small and big blinds can be skipped for a tag (a one-off bonus).

## Scoring
- Hand types from high card to straight flush, plus five of a kind, flush house and flush five with modified decks. Each type has base chips and mult and levels up with consumables. Score = (base chips + card chips + bonuses) x (base mult + bonuses) x multipliers, applied left to right, so the order of jokers matters.
- Card modifiers: enhancements, editions on cards and jokers, and stamps that trigger on play, hold or discard.

## Interface
- Left column: current blind and target, round score, chips and mult of the selected hand, hands and discards left, money, ante and round.
- Top: joker and consumable slots. Bottom: the hand, sortable by rank or suit, with play and discard buttons. Scoring animates card by card, with a number popping over each card and joker.

## Strategies and beginner mistakes
- Beginners buy many plus-chips jokers and too few multipliers, spend all their money (losing interest) and level many hand types instead of one. Good players pick one hand type, build mult early and multipliers late, keep money for interest and use discards to dig for their hand.

## Decisions for Ante Up
- A card-club theme of its own: green baize table under brass lamps; gem cards level hands, trick cards change playing cards, omen cards give strong effects with a cost.
- Own ante targets, hand values, prices and interest rule, and an original set of jokers, bosses, upgrades, tags, decks and stakes.

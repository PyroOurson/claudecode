# Reel Deep (fishing incremental)

Study of the genre (Tiny Fishing by Mad Buffer and similar hook-and-reel idle games), November 2026.

Loop: cast, the hook sinks; on the way down the player steers to avoid fish (touching one starts the reel-up early); at the line's maximum depth the hook comes back up and every fish it touches is caught, up to the hook's capacity. At the surface the catch is shown and sold. One cast lasts 10 to 30 seconds.

Upgrades: line length (depth), number of fish per cast, offline earnings per minute; each costs more. Deeper water holds rarer, more valuable fish; rare golden variants are worth many times more. No end: the pacing comes from new depths and species.

Controls: one button to cast, horizontal steering with mouse, finger or keys.

Feel: a splash on cast, bubbles and a depth counter on the way down, a satisfying pop per fish on the way up, a cash count-up when selling, a collection book of species.

Decisions: own species art and names (ordinary fish names), own boats, upgrades: line length, hook size, fish value, reel speed, offline earnings, an auto-fisher; a fish book with first-catch rewards.

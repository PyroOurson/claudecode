# Quack Cup (duck training and racing)

Study of the genre (Duck Life by Wix Games), November 2026.

Loop: the player raises a duck, trains its skills in short mini games (about 30 to 60 seconds each), earns coins, buys seeds to restore energy, then enters races. Races run on mixed terrain (track, water, hurdles, cliffs to climb, gaps to fly over); each skill sets the duck's speed on its terrain, so a race shows which skills are weak. Winning the tournaments of one region opens the next; the last is the championship.

Skills: running, swimming, flying, climbing, plus energy. Each training raises one skill's experience; levels show as bars. Energy drains in races and refills with food.

Controls: alternate two keys or tap to move, jump or flap with one key, steer up and down in water.

Interface: a hub (farm) with training spots, shop, races; an info card with skill levels; race results with places and prize coins.

Beginner mistakes: racing too early, training only running, ignoring energy.

Decisions: four skills and energy, original region and rival names, own duck art and hats, six tournaments of rising difficulty, all text and art original.

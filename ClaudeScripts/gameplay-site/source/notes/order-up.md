# Order Up (cooking and restaurant time management)

Study of the genre (the Papa's restaurant series by Flipline, plus Cooking Fever for several restaurants in one game), November 2026.

Session loop: a day opens with a short intro (day number, new ingredients, new customers), customers walk in, the player takes an order at the counter, a ticket goes on the rail, the food moves through 2 to 3 stations (build, cook with a timer, finish), then is served and scored. A day lasts 6 to 15 customers (about 5 to 8 minutes) and ends with a summary, rank points, unlocks and the shop.

Stations (one per screen, switched with tabs): order counter, assembly (sauce, cheese, toppings in the right places, or a stack in the ticket's order), cooking with a dial (bake until the bell, flip patties at half, blend to a level) running in the background while the player works elsewhere, finishing (cutting, toppings, cream).

Scoring: every station is graded 0 to 100 (waiting time, placement accuracy, cooking accuracy, finishing); the overall score sets the tip. Customers keep patience while they wait; slow service lowers the waiting score and very slow service makes them leave. Perfect orders give bonus tips and points.

Progression: rank points every day; new ingredients and customers unlock by day; the shop sells upgrades (more oven or grill space, timers, guides) and furniture or decorations that make customers wait longer. Difficulty rises with more customers, closer arrivals, longer orders, tighter patience. A special demanding customer arrives at the end of the day.

Several restaurants: Cooking Fever style, new restaurants unlock with the player's level; money is shared, each restaurant has its own days and upgrades.

Feel: bells and dings for cooking cues, cash register sound on tips, customers' faces change with patience, the ticket always visible at the stations, keys 1 to 4 for stations.

Beginner mistakes: forgetting food in the oven or on the grill, building before the cooking is done, taking too many orders at once, toppings in the wrong quarter.

Decisions: three restaurants (pizza: build, bake, cut; burger: grill with flips, build in order; shakes: build and fill, blend to a level, top), shared money and chef rank, per restaurant days, upgrades and decorations, a food critic each evening from day 2, all names, people, art and sounds original.

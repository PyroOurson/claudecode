# Long Shot

Rarity-scored daily trivia in the style of Krillion (owner's reference). Game id `long-shot`, part file `w/parts/307_g_long-shot.js`, generated by `python3 games/long-shot/build.py` from `games/long-shot/src/*.js` and the answer lists in `games/long-shot/data/*.txt`. The build also writes `w/new/i18n_b6.json` (interface rows from `games/long-shot/i18n_ui.json` plus one row per prompt).

## Study of the original (public guides and fan sites, November 2026)

- Session: seven prompts a day, the same for everyone, about 25 seconds each (one site says 15), so a daily round takes 3 to 4 minutes. One go per day; the board changes at a fixed time.
- Answering: type one thing that fits ("name a bird"). A refused answer costs only time and the prompt stays open; the first accepted answer locks in. Text still in the box when time runs out is submitted. Pass scores zero. Small typos and plurals are accepted.
- Scoring: six rarity tiers, from the obvious answer to the one almost nobody gives (10 to 100 points). Speed earns nothing; it only breaks ties in head-to-head play. Points become a depth (every point sinks you 10 m, perfect day 7,000 m) shown on an ocean dive with named zones.
- Results: score out of 700, zone reached, tier of each answer, an answer sheet with the most common and the rarest answers for each prompt, and a share card that shows tiers but never answers.
- Modes: endless (three air tanks, lose one on a miss or pass, the top tier gives one back), live rooms of up to 40 with the same prompt at the same moment and a reveal of everyone's answers after each prompt, private rooms, and ranked 1 v 1 (seven prompts, 15 seconds, more points wins, faster scoring breaks ties, rating starts at 1,000).
- Common strategy: skip the first answer that comes to mind, think one level more specific (a species, a small country), but keep a safe answer ready because a miss is worth nothing. Beginner mistakes: typing the most famous answer, or chasing an obscure one that is not in the list and running out of time.

## What this version does

- Theme of its own: a space probe launched from Earth. Every point is 0.1 AU (about 15 million km); the probe flies past Mars, the asteroid belt, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, the Kuiper Belt, Eris, the termination shock, the heliopause and Voyager 1. A typical day ends between Uranus and Neptune, a perfect day near Eris.
- Tiers (own names): Stardust 10 (10 % of players or more), Satellite 20 (4 %), Comet 35 (1.5 %), Nebula 55 (0.6 %), Supernova 80 (0.2 %), Long Shot 100 (under 0.2 %).
- Daily: 7 prompts, 25 s, a short grace before the clock moves, one go per UTC day (#1 is 13 November 2026). Progress is saved after every prompt; a prompt shown and not answered counts as a miss, so reloading cannot re-roll it. Endless: 3 fuel cells, a Long Shot refuels one. Duel: 7 prompts, 15 s, against a person (party of 2) or the computer (Easy, Medium, Hard, Hardest), Elo rating from 1,000. Live room: party of 2 to 16 (the portal caps parties at 16; the game already offers 24, 32 and 40 if the cap is raised), host picks 5 to 15 prompts and 15 to 30 s, bots fill empty seats.
- Answers: 63 curated sets, 8,800 answers, each with an English and a French name and aliases, a fame weight (0 to 9) and tags; 126 prompts (107 in the daily pool, dealt in 15-day cycles with no repeats and never two prompts of one family on the same day). Word prompts come in pairs: English players get "rhymes with light/day/cat/cake", French players get "ends in -eau/-ette/-oir/-age"; other languages get the English version. Answers are accepted in English and French whatever the interface language.
- Matching: lowercase, accents removed, punctuation and spaces ignored, leading articles dropped (the, a, le, la, l', un...), "St" read as "Saint", set-specific words dropped ("river", "lac", "mount"...), Roman numerals also as digits (Louis 14), plural endings, "ph" read as "f", then a typo allowance (1 letter from 5 letters, 2 from 9, first letter must match). A word that is an exact name anywhere in the 8,800 answers is never typo-corrected into a different answer (so Cambridge does not become Cambodia); it gets "That's real, but it doesn't fit", like a real answer from the same list that does not fit the prompt. Decoys tagged `no` exist only for that message.
- Moderation: typed text never leaves the device except to the room host, who checks it and sends back only list keys; other players only ever see the canonical name from the lists. Offensive words are refused before matching ("Keep it friendly"); the lists are checked against the same filter by the build.

## Server calls (database part in `long-shot.sql`)

The portal does not yet give games a way to call Supabase. The game looks for `Ameplay.account.rpc(name, args)` returning a promise (with `status`/`code` on errors). Suggested one-line addition to `Ameplay.account` in `w/new/auth.js`: `rpc(f, b) { return sbRest('rpc/' + f, { method: 'POST', body: b || {} }); }`. Without it, or when signed out, or when a call fails with 404 / `PGRST202` (function missing), the game uses the seed weights and shows "Live counts are offline".

- `long_shot_answer(p_prompt text, p_answer text) → {n, t, c}`: called by the player's own device for every accepted answer in every mode. Counts the answer once per player per prompt per UTC day (`c` = counted), at most 300 counts per player per day and 100 calls per 10 minutes (`private.rate_ok`), at most 2,000 distinct answers per prompt. Returns the count of that answer `n` and the prompt's total `t`. Signed-in users only; locked accounts refused.
- `long_shot_stats(p_prompts text[]) → [{p, t, a: [[answer, n] × 40]}]`: up to 10 prompts, the 40 most common answers and the total. Anyone may call it (rate limited). Used at the start of a round (rarity for rooms and bots, "Most said" on the results).
- Prompt keys are the prompt ids (`country-af`); a word pair adds the language (`w-light.fr`). Answer keys are the slug of the English name (`ivory-coast`).
- Rarity = (200 × seed share + live count) / (200 + live total): the curated weights act as 200 imaginary answers, so live counts take over as players answer.
- Stored: counts per prompt and answer (no user), totals, and `long_shot_seen (day, uid, prompt)` for the once-a-day rule, deleted after two days. No answer is ever stored with a user id.

## Maintainer notes

- Add answers or prompts in `data/`, run `build.py`; it refuses duplicate ids, two answers sharing a matching key, offensive names and prompts with fewer than 8 answers. Adding prompts reshuffles the daily cycle.
- Bots: Easy, Medium, Hard, Hardest differ in miss rate, answer time and how strongly they prefer rare answers (`LS_BOTS`). Simulated averages per prompt: 20, 37, 55, 79 points; Hardest beat Hard in 1,941 of 2,000 duels.
- Room tie-breaks use the time from the prompt start on the host, minus half the guest's ping.

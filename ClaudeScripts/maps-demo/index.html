What this is

      
maps-server is a public-transport routing engine. This page runs its route search and its map loader, compiled to WebAssembly, on the OpenStreetMap data of Monaco. Nothing is sent to a server: the map tiles are the only thing downloaded while you use it.

      
The bus times are not real. The lines and their stops come from OpenStreetMap, but the timetable is made up for this demo: a bus every 10 minutes from 06:00 to 22:00, Monaco time, at about 18 km/h. The real server gets its times from transit plugins, such as the SNCF one, which cannot run in a browser.

      
Times are shown in your own time zone. The real server also needs Docker, Overpass and its plugins; see its README.
